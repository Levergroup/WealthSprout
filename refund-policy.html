1. Our 30-Day Guarantee

We stand behind every WealthSprout product with a 30-day satisfaction guarantee.

If you are not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, contact us within 30 days of your purchase date at hello@wealthsproutkids.com and we will issue a full refund. No questions asked.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Email hello@wealthsproutkids.com

We will process your refund within 3–5 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Because our products are digital downloads delivered immediately upon purchase, we rely on our 30-day satisfaction guarantee rather than a traditional return process. You do not need to "return" a digital product to receive a refund under our guarantee.

Refunds are processed through Paddle, our payment processor. Depending on your bank or card issuer, refunds may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement after we process them.

We reserve the right to decline refund requests that show evidence of abuse of our refund policy — for example, repeated purchases and refund requests of the same product.

We encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. We are committed to resolving any issues promptly and our 30-day guarantee covers all purchases. Initiating a chargeback without contacting us first may result in your account being flagged.
