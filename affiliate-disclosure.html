Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

WealthSprout participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means that if you click certain links on our Site or in our emails and sign up for or purchase a product, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This disclosure is provided in compliance with the FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255).

1. Overview

We recommend a small number of third-party financial products that we believe are genuinely useful for families teaching kids about money. When you click one of these links and sign up or make a purchase, the partner company may pay WealthSprout a referral commission. This never changes the price you pay.

2. Our Affiliate Partners

  • Greenlight — a debit card and money app for kids and teens with parental controls. We may earn a commission if you sign up for Greenlight through our link.
  • Fidelity Youth Account — a teen-owned brokerage account from Fidelity with parental oversight. We may earn a commission if you open an account through our link.
  • Acorns Early — a custodial investment account designed to help parents invest on behalf of their kids. We may earn a commission if you sign up through our link.
  • Stockpile — a platform that lets families buy fractional shares of stock as gifts or for custodial investing. We may earn a commission if you sign up through our link.

3. How We Choose What to Recommend

We select affiliate partners based on our own genuine assessment of product quality and educational fit for the age groups we serve. Our affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial opinions, the content of our workbooks, or which concepts we choose to teach — our curriculum is built first, and any product mentions are added only where they're a natural, honest fit.

4. Pricing & Availability

Pricing, account features, and availability for any affiliate partner are controlled entirely by that company and may change at any time without notice to us. Please review each partner's own terms, fees, and disclosures directly on their website before signing up.

5. Future Affiliate Relationships

We may add new affiliate relationships over time, including with other financial education tools, apps, or family finance products. Any new affiliate relationship will be disclosed on this page, which we encourage you to revisit periodically.

6. FTC Compliance

This disclosure is intended to comply with the FTC's endorsement guidelines under 16 CFR Part 255, which require clear disclosure of any material connection between an endorser and the marketer of a product.
