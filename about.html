About WealthSprout

The financial education school forgot — built for families who want more.

Our Story

WealthSprout was founded by Maya Hartwell, a former middle school math teacher and mother of two. After spending eleven years in the classroom teaching concepts that would serve students for a lifetime — and watching personal finance remain completely absent from every curriculum she was given — she decided to build the resource she could never find.

Maya paid off $31,000 in debt at 35 and opened her first Roth IRA at 37. None of it was taught to her. She figured it out the hard way — from podcasts, spreadsheets, and expensive mistakes. When her oldest started asking questions about money, she realized there was nothing that felt right for a real family: not babyish, not a textbook, not a YouTube rabbit hole.

So she built one. WealthSprout is the financial education Maya wished existed when she was eight. And sixteen. And thirty.

Our Mission

To give every child the financial vocabulary, habits, and tools that the school system forgot to teach — so they start adulthood ahead.

What We Build

WealthSprout creates age-matched financial literacy workbooks for children ages 5–18. Every program is designed to be used by parents and children together — not as homework, but as shared discovery. Our programs cover earning, saving, giving, budgeting, investing, and building real wealth — in age-appropriate language that respects children's intelligence.
