Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies WealthSprout ("we," "us," "our") uses on wealthsprout.com (the "Site"), and how you can manage your preferences. This Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow a site to recognize your browser, remember preferences, and collect information about how the site is used. We also use similar technologies such as tracking pixels (e.g., the Meta Pixel) and local storage.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep our Site and checkout working correctly, to understand how visitors use our Site so we can improve it, and to show relevant advertising to people who may be interested in our programs — including on other websites and social platforms.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary — required for the Site to function, including maintaining your session, signing in to your account, and completing checkout via Stripe. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics — help us understand how visitors use the Site (e.g., Google Analytics) so we can improve content and performance. These are only set if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Marketing / Advertising — used for ad targeting, conversion tracking, and remarketing, including the Meta Pixel and Google Ads conversion/remarketing tags. These are only set if you accept marketing cookies.
  • Functional — remember your preferences, such as language or display settings, to personalize your experience.

4. Cookie Consent

On your first visit to the Site, a cookie consent banner lets you "Accept All" cookies or "Manage Preferences" to individually enable or disable Analytics and Marketing cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are always active because the Site cannot function correctly without them. We do not load analytics or advertising scripts — including Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel — until you have given consent for the relevant category. Your choice is stored in your browser and you can update it at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this Site and revisiting, or by using the preference controls referenced below.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site are placed by third parties, including:

  • Google (Google Analytics, Google Ads) — see Google's cookie policy.
  • Meta / Facebook (Meta Pixel) — see Meta's cookie policy.
  • Stripe — used solely to support secure checkout and fraud prevention.

6. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can manage Analytics and Marketing cookies at any time through our cookie consent banner. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or delete cookies after each session. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent checkout and account features from working correctly. You can additionally opt out of personalized advertising directly through:

  • Google: adssettings.google.com
  • Meta/Facebook: facebook.com/ads/preferences

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies and tracking technologies we use. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

8. Contact Us
