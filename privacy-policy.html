Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Blue Tree Group LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), operating under the brand name WealthSprout at wealthsproutkids.com ("Site"), collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit our Site, purchase our products, or subscribe to our email list.

By using our Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly:

  • Name and email address when you subscribe to our free kit or email list
  • Name, email address, and payment information when you purchase a product
  • Any information you provide when contacting us

Information Collected Automatically:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring URLs
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

Payment Information:

All payment processing is handled by Paddle (paddle.com), our Merchant of Record. Blue Tree Group LLC does not collect, store, or process credit card numbers or financial account information. Paddle's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver digital products you have purchased
  • Send you the free resources you requested
  • Send email marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Improve our website and products
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Process and fulfill orders through Paddle

3. Email Marketing

When you subscribe to our email list or download a free resource, you consent to receive email communications from WealthSprout. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You may opt out at any time.

We use GetResponse as our email marketing provider. Your email address and name are stored in GetResponse's systems in accordance with their privacy policy.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only with:

  • Paddle — to process payments and fulfill orders
  • GetResponse — to deliver email communications
  • Vercel — to host and deliver our website
  • Google Analytics — to understand site usage

All third-party service providers are required to protect your information in accordance with applicable law.

5. Cookies

Our Site uses cookies to:

  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns (Google Analytics)
  • Remember your preferences
  • Support marketing and retargeting features

You may disable cookies in your browser settings. Some features of the Site may not function properly if cookies are disabled. See our Cookie Policy for full detail.

6. Children's Privacy

Our products are designed for use by parents and educators with children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at hello@wealthsproutkids.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@wealthsproutkids.com.

9. Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.
