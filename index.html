What Kids Actually Need to Know

Most curriculums stop at long division and never circle back to the math that shows up later on a paycheck, a credit card statement, or a brokerage account.

Here's what's usually missing:

  • How compound interest actually works
  • What a credit score is and why it matters
  • How to read a paycheck or build a budget
  • What a stock, index fund, or Roth IRA is
  • How debt — good and bad — actually works
