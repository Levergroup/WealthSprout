Last Updated: June 22, 2026

WealthSprout runs paid advertising to let more families know about our programs. This Advertising Disclosure explains how we advertise, the platforms we use, and how you can control the ads you see.

1. Overview

We advertise on Google (Search and Display Network) and on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). These platforms may use cookies and similar technologies to show you ads based on your visit to our Site, your interaction with our ads, or interests inferred by the platform. See our Cookie Policy for how we gate this with your consent.

2. Google Ads

  • We run paid advertising campaigns on the Google Search Network and Google Display Network. Sponsored results and display ads from WealthSprout are clearly identified as ads by Google's own ad labeling.
  • Remarketing: if you visit our Site, Google's remarketing technology may allow us to show you WealthSprout ads later as you browse other websites in the Google Display Network.
  • You can opt out of personalized Google ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregated, anonymized traffic and campaign performance. GA4 does not identify you personally.

3. Meta / Facebook Ads

  • We run paid advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram, clearly labeled "Sponsored" by Meta's own platform conventions.
  • Custom Audiences: we may use email addresses or website visit data (collected via the Meta Pixel) to build Custom Audiences, allowing us to show ads to past visitors, customers, or people with similar interests ("Lookalike Audiences").
  • You can opt out of personalized Meta/Facebook ads at facebook.com/ads/preferences.
  • Meta Pixel: our Site uses the Meta Pixel, which places a cookie in your browser to help us measure ad performance and build the audiences described above. The Pixel only loads after you consent to marketing cookies.

4. General Advertising Practices

We aim to comply with FTC guidelines on advertising and endorsements:

  • Sponsored content and paid promotions are clearly labeled as such.
  • Testimonials and reviews featured in our advertising reflect the genuine experiences of real customers. Individual results vary, and a testimonial is not a guarantee that you or your child will have the same experience.
  • We do not use deceptive or misleading claims in our advertising.

5. Our "Free" Offers

Some of our advertising promotes the My First Money Kit as a "free" offer. This is accurate: the Money Kit costs nothing, no processing or delivery fee applies, and no credit card is required to receive it.
