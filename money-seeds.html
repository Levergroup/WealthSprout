IS THIS RIGHT FOR YOUR CHILD?

Built for Emma, Age 6.

She loves stickers, drawing, and being in charge of things. She has a piggy bank but doesn't really understand why. Money Seeds is built for exactly where she is right now.

This is for your child if:

  • They're ages 5–8 and just starting to understand money
  • They have a piggy bank but no real system behind it
  • You want them to build saving habits before bad spending habits form
  • You want to teach them without needing a finance background yourself

This is NOT for your child if:

  • They're already comfortable with saving, budgeting, and the jar system

Money Seeds FAQ — Kid's First Financial Playbook Questions

Money Seeds is built for early readers. Every concept uses pictures, simple sentences, and short activities, with a few pages made to do together with a parent reading aloud. Most kids ages 5–8 can do the majority of it on their own by the second half of the book.

That's fine — the book doesn't assume any allowance. Chapter 1 walks you through small ways to give your child their first money to manage, even if it's just birthday cash or a few dollars for a chore, so the jars have something real to hold from day one.

Money Seeds is a digital PDF delivered instantly to your inbox after purchase. Use it on a phone, tablet, or laptop, or print it at home — it's yours to keep either way.

Money Seeds covers ages 5–8. When your child is ready for more — budgeting, earning, and their first business idea — Money Moves picks up right where this leaves off, for ages 9–12.

A mix of both by design. Most pages work for your child to do on their own once they're reading independently, but a handful — like setting up the first savings goal — work best when you sit down together for a few minutes.
